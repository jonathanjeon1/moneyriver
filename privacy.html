Money River

Your numbers stay with you

Money River never sees what you type. There are no accounts, no ads, no trackers and no cookies.

Nothing is sent anywhere

All the math happens in your browser. The page is also locked by a security setting (a Content Security Policy) that blocks it from sending data to any server, including ours.

Where your answers are kept

Only in the browser tab you're using, so a refresh doesn't lose your place. Close the tab and they're gone. If you turn on "Remember my answers on this device", they're kept in that browser until you tap Start over, and nobody else can see them.

Downloads

Your plan PDF, net worth image and Word file are made right in your browser and saved straight to your device.

What our web host sees

Like every website, the service that hosts this page receives basic technical details when it loads, such as your IP address and browser type. It never receives the numbers you enter.

If this ever changes

If we add visit counting or partner links, this page will say so first, and your numbers will still never leave your device.
